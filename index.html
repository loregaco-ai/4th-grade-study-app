American English listening practice

Choose a US English voice installed on your device. Tap Play to hear a question and its answer choices. For car practice, use Hands-free practice to hear the answer after a pause.

Ready

The voice quality depends on the US English voices available in your browser. On iPhone, open the hosted app in Safari for the most reliable audio playback. Audio may stop when the phone locks; keep the screen awake during car practice. Audio uses built-in speech synthesis, not a downloaded recording.
